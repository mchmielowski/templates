The First Day Guide

Mini Yoga & Zumba Kids

A playful mix of gentle stretching, rhythm, and dance to help children feel strong and free.
Children move, stretch, and dance their way through joyful classes designed to build confidence, coordination, and calm. Every rhythm, every pose, every giggle — a celebration of movement and being.

About the Class

‍

At Bobolobo, we believe movement is more than exercise — it’s communication.

Mini Yoga & Zumba Kids helps little ones express emotions through rhythm and breath, teaching them to listen to their bodies and enjoy mindful motion.

Our certified instructors blend fun choreography with gentle stretching, using music and imagination to keep children engaged and happy.

Classes are filled with songs, stories, and spontaneous laughter — never competition or pressure.

What They Learn

‍

  • Body awareness: discovering balance, strength, and coordination.
  • Rhythm & focus: moving with music while building attention and flow.
  • Emotional expression: releasing energy in safe, positive ways.
  • Mindfulness through play: calm breathing and soft relaxation moments.

Typical Class Flow

‍

9:00 AM — Warm-up & breathing games 

Gentle stretching, songs, and storytelling to set the tone.

‍

9:15 AM — Zumba fun! 

Upbeat rhythms, group dances, and playful moves inspired by Latin beats.

‍

9:40 AM — Mini Yoga & wind-down 

Quiet poses, balance exercises, and guided relaxation with music.
