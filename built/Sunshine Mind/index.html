Frequently asked questions
01
What therapy do you offer?

We offer a range of therapy options tailored to individual needs, including cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), mindfulness-based therapy, and couples counseling. Our focus is on providing modern, effective treatment modalities suitable for issues like anxiety, depression, relationship challenges, and more.

02
How does the process work?

We begin with a free 15-minute discovery call to understand your goals and match you with the right therapist. From there, you'll book your first full session — available in person or over secure video — and work together to set clear, meaningful intentions. Most clients meet weekly, and your therapist will regularly check in on your progress to keep the support evolving alongside you.

03
Who are your typical clients?

Our clients are adults from all walks of life — busy professionals managing burnout, parents navigating relationship strain, and individuals working through anxiety, grief, or past trauma. You don't need to be in crisis to benefit. Many people come to us simply because they want a trusted, compassionate space to reflect, grow, and feel more like themselves.

04
How can I get started?

It's easier than you might think. Fill in our short online form to request a free discovery call, or contact us by phone or email. A member of our team will be in touch within one business day to discuss your needs and recommend the right therapist for you. From there, booking your first session takes just a few minutes.
