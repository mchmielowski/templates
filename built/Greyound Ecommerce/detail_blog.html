Effective strategic planning begins not with a slide deck but with a genuine understanding of where the business stands today. Too often, organisations invest in expensive strategy processes that produce ambitious frameworks with little connection to operational reality. The most impactful engagements we run at Greyhound start with listening — to leadership, to frontline teams, and to the data that often tells a different story from the one in the boardroom. When strategy is grounded in honest diagnosis, execution becomes significantly more straightforward, and the results have a durability that purely top-down plans rarely achieve.
