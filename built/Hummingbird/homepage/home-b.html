FAQ

What types of memberships do you offer?

We offer flexible membership options to fit your needs, including day passes, dedicated desks, private offices, and virtual services. Whether you need a spot for a day or a permanent base, Hummingbird has you covered.

Are there any additional benefits to joining Hummingbird?

Absolutely! Members get access to exclusive workshops, networking events, and wellness programs. Plus, enjoy perks like discounts at local cafes and shops, and free entry to guest speaker events.

Can I bring guests to the coworking space?

Yes, members are welcome to bring guests. We provide guest passes which can be purchased at a discounted rate. It’s a great way to introduce your collaborators to the Hummingbird community.

What makes Hummingbird different from other coworking spaces?

Hummingbird is uniquely designed for Gen Z women, blending a retro aesthetic with modern facilities. Our focus on sustainability, creativity, and community sets us apart, creating a space where you can work, learn, and grow in harmony with your values.
