Description

Introducing our latest merch masterpiece: a sleek and comfortable essential that effortlessly blends style and comfort. Elevate your wardrobe with this must-have piece, designed for those who appreciate quality and fashion-forward flair.

product details

Crafted from 100% organic ring-spun cotton, this piece is preshrunk and garment-washed for a soft, broken-in feel from day one. The relaxed cut allows for easy layering, and the reinforced shoulder seams ensure it holds its shape wash after wash. Available in sizes S–XXL. Printed using water-based inks that are kind to the environment and built to last.

use instruction

Wear as a standalone statement piece or layer under an open jacket. The neutral palette makes it easy to pair with denim, tailored trousers, or relaxed separates. For printed areas, turn the garment inside-out before putting it on to keep the artwork looking its best over time.

washing & care

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with like colours. Do not bleach or tumble dry — lay flat or hang to dry. If ironing is needed, turn inside out and use a low heat setting, avoiding the printed areas. Following these steps will keep the colour vivid and the fabric feeling great for years of wear.
