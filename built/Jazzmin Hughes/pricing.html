How do I place an order?

Browse the shop or pricing packages, select the items or services that suit your needs, and click "Buy Now." You will be guided through a secure checkout process where you can review your selection, enter your shipping details, and complete payment. Once your order is confirmed you will receive an email receipt. For custom commissions or service packages, feel free to reach out via the contact page before purchasing so we can tailor the brief together.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways. For service packages or commissioned work, we can also arrange bank transfer — just get in touch and we will send over the details.

What is your return policy?

Physical goods (prints, apparel, and merchandise) can be returned within 14 days of delivery, provided they are unused and in their original packaging. Digital products and completed commissions are non-refundable once delivered. If something arrives damaged or not as described, please contact us within 48 hours and we will arrange a replacement or full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide. International orders are fulfilled within 5–10 business days, depending on your location. Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and parcel weight. Please note that customs duties or import taxes are the buyer's responsibility and vary by country. We use tracked couriers for all international shipments so you can follow your order from studio to doorstep.
