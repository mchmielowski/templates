Crafting Dreams

This project began as a commission to reimagine the visual language of a long-running creative institution. The brief called for something that honoured the archive while speaking clearly to a new generation — a balancing act that shaped every decision from the initial mood-boarding sessions through to the final print production. Working closely with the client over four months, we developed a system built around a restricted palette, a single expressive typeface, and a grid loose enough to feel alive. The result is an identity that breathes differently across different applications: bold and declarative on large-format posters, intimate and precise on printed matter, and fluid on screen.
