Summary

Join David Chord for an unforgettable journey through the musical highlights of 2024. This special year-end episode of Sound Stories takes you from the year’s groundbreaking hits to the chart-topping albums that defined the sound of the past 12 months.

‍

David is joined by the renowned music critic and industry expert Antuan Fontanto, who offers his unique perspective on the trends, triumphs, and surprises that shaped the world of music this year. Together, they break down the moments that left us speechless, the songs that became anthems, and the artists who pushed the boundaries of creativity.
