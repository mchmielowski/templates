This podcast isn’t just about music—it’s about the life in music.
Where music meets storytelling and every note has a tale to tell. The David Chord Podcast is your backstage pass to the rhythms that shape our lives. Each week, we dive deep into the beats, melodies, and voices that resonate with us, exploring not just the music itself but the stories, cultures, and experiences that create it.
