About
The Host

As the voice behind Sound Stories, he’s here to uncover the beats, creators, & unforgettable moments.

David Chord
Dave’s love for music started with a guitar and grew through years of exploration, jam sessions, and late-night conversations. His journey led him to realize that every song has a story—stories of life, struggle, and triumph. Now, as the host of Sound Stories, Dave uncovers the beats behind the music and the creators who make them unforgettable. Join him on a journey where music meets life.
