Requirements

Who are you?

  • 3+ years of backend or fullstack (front-end app) development experience

  • Academic degree in Computer Science (or related field) preferred

  • Enjoy flexible working hours and holidays wishes (paid and extra unpaid leave)
  • Excellent compensation package
