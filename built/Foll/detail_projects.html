Bloom & Vine — Floral Brand Identity

A complete visual identity and photography system for a Brooklyn flower studio — from wordmark and palette to the campaign imagery that carries it across every shopfront and screen.

Bloom & Vine came to us with a beautiful product and no language to describe it. We built that language from the ground up: a soft serif wordmark, a palette pulled from dried petals and eucalyptus, and a photographic style that treats every bouquet like a portrait. The goal was a brand that felt hand-arranged rather than mass-produced — quiet, tactile, and unmistakably theirs.

"Foll gave our flowers a voice. People recognise us now before they even read the name."

With the identity in place, we rolled it across every touchpoint: kraft sleeves and ribbon, a refreshed shopfront, care cards, and a seasonal lookbook shot entirely in natural light. Six months after launch, Bloom & Vine had doubled their wedding bookings and built a waitlist for their weekly market drops.
