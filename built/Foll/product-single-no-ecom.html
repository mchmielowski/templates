Description

A midweight brushed-fleece crewneck in soft sage, finished with a tonal embroidered Foll mark at the chest. Dropped shoulders and a relaxed body give it that easy, lived-in feel from day one.

80% organic cotton, 20% recycled polyester, 380gsm loopback fleece. Cut and sewn in limited runs, with ribbed cuffs and hem that hold their shape wash after wash.

A studio staple layered over a tee or worn on its own. Sized unisex with a roomy fit — size down for a closer cut.
