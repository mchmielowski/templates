FAQ

①
What kind of projects does Foll take on?

Foll works across brand identity, art direction, packaging and editorial photography. Most engagements start with a visual strategy, then move into design and a dedicated shoot so the imagery and the brand system feel like one piece.

②
How long does a typical project take?

A focused identity or campaign usually runs four to six weeks, while a full brand build with photography can take two to three months. I keep the studio intentionally small, so you work directly with me from first sketch to final delivery.

③
Do you offer both design and photography together?

Yes — combining the two is what Foll is built around. Shooting in-house means the photography is designed alongside the typography, color and layout rather than sourced afterwards, which keeps every touchpoint consistent.

④
How do we start working together?

Send a short note through the contact page with your timeline and what you are building. We will set up an intro call, outline scope and a fixed quote, and once that is agreed the studio blocks time in the calendar and the project kicks off.
