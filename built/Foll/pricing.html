FAQ

①
What services does Foll offer?

We work across brand identity, art direction, packaging, and both editorial and product photography — usually as a single, joined-up engagement so the design and the images speak the same language.

②
How long does a typical project take?

Most identity projects run four to eight weeks; a standalone photo shoot can be turned around in a week or two. We map out a clear timeline together before anything begins.

③
Do you work with clients outside New York?

Yes. Roughly half of our clients are based outside the city. We run discovery sessions and reviews remotely, and travel for on-location shoots when a project calls for it.

④
How do we get started?

Send us a note through the contact page with a little about your project. We'll set up an intro call, scope the work together, and share a proposal within a few days.
