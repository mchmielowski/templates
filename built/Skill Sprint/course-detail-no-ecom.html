How long do I have access to this course?

Once enrolled, you have lifetime access to all course materials including future updates. Learn at whatever pace suits your schedule — revisit lessons as often as you need, and pick up right where you left off across any device.

Do I need any prior experience to enroll?

Each course page lists the recommended prerequisites clearly. Many of our courses are designed for beginners with no background required. For intermediate and advanced tracks, we suggest reviewing the prerequisites section and completing our free starter lessons to gauge the right fit.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes — every course awards a shareable completion certificate once you finish all lessons and pass the final assessment. You can download it as a PDF or share it directly to your LinkedIn profile to showcase your new skills to employers and peers.

What if I'm not satisfied with the course?

We stand behind the quality of every course on Skill Sprint. If you're not happy within the first 14 days of enrollment, contact our support team for a full refund — no questions asked. Our goal is for you to walk away with real, applicable skills you can use immediately.
