Brand Identity · 2026

Lumen Skincare

Lumen came to us as a fast-growing skincare line with a loyal following but a fragmented look — packaging, social, and retail displays all felt like they belonged to different companies. Our brief was to build one identity that could carry the brand from boutique shelves to a national rollout without losing its calm, clinical warmth.

We rebuilt the system around a soft, light-led palette and a precise editorial typeface, paired with a flexible grid that scales from a single product label to a full campaign spread. In the six months after launch, Lumen reported a 38% lift in repeat purchases and secured placement in three national retailers — proof that a coherent identity is a growth lever, not just a coat of paint.
