Filters on careers page

Because we are not allowed to use custom code in our templates, some elements are not fully functional and are prepared to be later used in combination with custom code. This is why the filters on the careers page are not fully functional. However, you can enable them by using one of the third-party plugins.

We suggest using Refokus CMS Filters due to its ease of implementation. Simply follow the steps on their website to make the filters work on your site.
