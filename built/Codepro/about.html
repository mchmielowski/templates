Introducing our team of leaders
Daniel Brooks
Founder & CEO
With more than 18 years building software companies, Daniel founded Codepro on a simple belief: great engineering and honest partnership ship products that last. He has led the delivery of bespoke platforms, SaaS products, and AI systems for startups and enterprises across fintech, healthcare, and logistics. Daniel works closely with every engagement team to keep quality, transparency, and long-term client outcomes at the center of how Codepro builds.
Priya Nair
Chief Technology Officer
Priya leads Codepro's technology strategy and engineering standards across every project. A systems architect by background, she has designed scalable cloud platforms, event-driven microservices, and production machine-learning pipelines for high-growth products. Priya champions clean architecture, rigorous code review, and pragmatic technology choices, ensuring the systems we ship stay maintainable and ready to scale long after launch.
Marcus Feld
Head of Engineering
Marcus runs Codepro's delivery teams and engineering practice. He has shipped dozens of web portals, mobile apps, and custom platforms, and is obsessed with developer experience, automated testing, and reliable CI/CD. Marcus mentors our engineers, sets the bar for code quality, and makes sure every release reaches production smoothly, predictably, and on schedule.
Elena Vasquez
Product Lead
Elena bridges our clients' vision and what we build. As Product Lead she runs discovery, shapes roadmaps, and turns complex requirements into clear, user-centered products. With a background in UX research and agile delivery, she keeps teams focused on outcomes that matter, ensuring every feature we ship solves a real problem for real users.

Who are we?
Codepro is a global group of engineers and creative thinkers who collaborate with customers to revolutionize entire sectors.
Our commitment and expertise distinguish us as a reliable partner, able to help bring a company's ideas to life, from concept to public offering, and even shape new industry standards.
What motivates us?
Our purpose is to connect technology leaders with the best talent to help them achieve their goals faster, drive innovation more effectively, and grow their businesses to unprecedented levels.
What drives us to function?
Codepro teams are committed to supporting our clients' vision and are equally passionate about the software we provide. With over 20 years of experience, we are dedicated to helping innovators bring their ideas to life, creating products that enhance the lives of users and revolutionize industries on a large scale.
