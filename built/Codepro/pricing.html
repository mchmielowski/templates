Hourly Rate and Costs
We operate on a Time & Materials basis, meaning the cost of any project or development will be determined by the amount of work required and the hourly rate of our team members.

Estimation and Team Makeup
Prior to beginning the project, we offer a rough estimate detailing the range of time needed for each feature of the application. We also present various team configurations, along with an initial monthly and overall project budget.
