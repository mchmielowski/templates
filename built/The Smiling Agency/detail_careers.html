Senior Brand Designer

We're looking for a Senior Brand Designer who believes good work should make people feel something. You'll lead identity projects from first sketch to final guidelines, shaping logos, type systems, and the little moments of delight that make a brand memorable.

You'll partner closely with our strategists and copywriters, present confidently to clients, and mentor two junior designers. We expect five-plus years in branding, a portfolio that shows range, and a healthy appetite for puns.

In return you get four-day weeks every summer, a generous learning budget, and a studio full of people who genuinely like showing up. Sound like you? We'd love to meet.
