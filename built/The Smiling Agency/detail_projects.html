BRANDING · 2026

Sunny Co. Rebrand

Sunny Co. came to us with a great product and a forgettable face. The juice was bright, the team was lovely, but the brand on the shelf felt like everyone else's. Our job was to bottle the feeling you get on the first warm morning of the year and put it on every touchpoint.

We rebuilt the identity from the ground up: a rounder wordmark, a palette pulled straight from a sunrise, and a tone of voice that talks to people like friends rather than customers. The new packaging hit shelves in March.

The results spoke for themselves. Within a quarter, shelf pickup rose 34%, social mentions tripled, and Sunny Co. landed its first national retail listing. Most importantly, the founders finally have a brand that feels like them: warm, generous, and impossible to walk past without smiling.
