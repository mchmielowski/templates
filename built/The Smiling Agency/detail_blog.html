Why a Smile Is the Best Brand Strategy

Every brand wants to be remembered, but few stop to ask how they want to make people feel. At The Smiling Agency we start with warmth. A smile is the shortest distance between a company and the human on the other side of the screen, and it turns out to be one of the most underrated tools in a strategist's kit.

When we rebranded Sunny Co. last spring, we resisted the urge to lead with features. Instead we built the identity around a single feeling: the quiet relief of being looked after. The logo got rounder, the copy got friendlier, and conversion climbed 34% in the first quarter. Joy, it turns out, is good business.

So before you debate another shade of blue or argue over a tagline, try a simpler test. Read your homepage out loud and notice whether it makes you grin. If it doesn't, you have your first brief.
