Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do you specialize in?

We work primarily with mid-market companies in manufacturing, industrial services, consumer goods, financial services, and technology. While every sector has its quirks, the underlying challenges — margin pressure, operational complexity, and the need to grow profitably — are remarkably consistent, and that is where our experience compounds.

How do you tailor your services to fit my business needs?

Every engagement begins with a structured diagnostic. We spend the first weeks understanding your data, your people, and your goals before recommending anything. From there we build a scope that fits your budget, your timeline, and your team's capacity — there is no off-the-shelf package that we force you into.

What is your typical timeline for a consulting project?

Most engagements run between eight and sixteen weeks, depending on scope. A focused diagnostic can be completed in four weeks, while a full transformation program may extend over several quarters. We always agree on clear milestones up front so you know exactly what to expect and when.

Do you offer ongoing support after the project is completed?

Yes. While our goal is always to build the internal capability for you to sustain results independently, many clients keep us on a lighter retainer for quarterly reviews, coaching, and a second set of eyes when new challenges arise. We are happy to flex our involvement up or down as your needs change.

How do you measure success?

We define success in your terms before the work begins — whether that is cost reduction, revenue growth, faster cycle times, or improved retention — and we tie our recommendations directly to those metrics. Throughout the engagement we track progress against a clear baseline so the impact is visible and accountable.
