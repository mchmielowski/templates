Frequently asked questions
How do I get started

Getting started is simple. Reach out through our contact form or book a discovery call, and we will scope your project, share a timeline and quote, and have you onboarded within a few business days.

What security measures are in place to protect my data?

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, enforce role-based access controls, and run regular penetration tests. All infrastructure is hosted with SOC 2 compliant providers and continuously monitored for threats.

Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Yes. Our team builds against well-documented APIs and has experience connecting with CRMs, ERPs, payment gateways, and custom internal tools. We will map your integrations during the discovery phase.

How does your 24/7 customer support work?

Our support engineers are available around the clock via email and chat. Priority plans include a dedicated Slack channel and a guaranteed response time so critical issues are never left waiting.

How does your 30-day free trial work?"

You get full access to every feature for 30 days with no credit card required. If it is not the right fit, simply walk away. If you love it, your plan begins automatically when the trial ends.

Is customer support available for all subscription plans?

Every plan includes access to our help center and email support. Growth and Enterprise plans add priority queues, a dedicated account manager, and faster response SLAs.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription plan?

Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time from your account dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.
