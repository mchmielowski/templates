Consulting Analyst

Location:
Amsterdam
Type:
Full-time

Job Description

As a Consulting Analyst, you will play a pivotal role in our consultancy team, contributing to research, data analysis, and market studies that inform strategic decision-making. Your insights will directly impact client recommendations, supporting our commitment to delivering informed and effective solutions.

‍

‍

The best analysts here are equal parts detective and storyteller. They dig into messy data, find the signal, and turn it into a recommendation a client can act on the same afternoon.

‍

‍

Your role

You will own end-to-end analysis for two or three client engagements at a time, from gathering and cleaning data to building models and presenting findings. Expect to run workshops, validate hypotheses with stakeholders, and translate numbers into clear, decision-ready narratives.

‍

Your team

You will join a tight-knit consultancy pod of six: two senior strategists, a data engineer, a designer, and a fellow analyst, led by a principal who has shipped work for fintech and logistics clients across Europe. We pair often, review each other's work openly, and celebrate the wins together.

‍

Requirements

‍

Who are you?

  • 3+ years of backend or fullstack (front-end app) development experience
  • Highly proficient with our stack
  • Academic degree in Computer Science (or related field) preferred
  • Proactive and ambitious engineer with good communication skills that loves contributing to international success in a diverse environment

‍

Benefits

  • Flourish in a culture of trust, ownership, and standard of excellence
  • Getting-things-done environment with sharp and knowledgeable colleagues
  • Minimal time spent in meetings
  • Speak English in a pan-European company
  • Work (partly or fully) remote whenever you want.......or in one of our lovely office hubs in Amsterdam, London, Frankfurt or Paris
  • The best equipment needed for your job, including MacBook Pro, iPhone, etc.Enjoy flexible working hours and holidays wishes (paid and extra unpaid leave)
  • Excellent compensation package
  • Company-wide outings & events

‍
