Project Overview

Chromatic Horizons was a season-long exhibition for Lumen Gallery built around the bold colour fields of a new generation of abstract painters. We developed the full visual identity — from the catalogue and signage to the digital invitations — and designed a viewing journey that let each canvas breathe. The result was a cohesive, unmistakably contemporary show that drew the gallery's largest opening-week audience to date.
