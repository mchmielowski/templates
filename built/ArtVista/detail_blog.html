The Art Trends Shaping Galleries in 2024

Every year the art world redraws its own boundaries, and 2024 is no exception. Across galleries, studios and digital platforms, a new visual language is taking shape — one that blends tactile craft with bold experimentation. From the soft, sculptural feel of neomorphism to the unapologetic energy of abstract expressionism, artists are rejecting the flat and the formulaic in favour of work that invites you to look twice. Below we explore the movements and materials defining the season at ArtVista, and how collectors and creators alike can make the most of them.

Design evolves with neomorphism, abstract art, artistic typography with more sustainability, and 3D immersion. Let's paint a dynamic and more engaging future.
Stay ahead of the curve and embrace these design trends to breathe new life into your brand creative projects. As designers, our mission is to constantly innovate and deliver captivating experiences. The year 2024 promises a canvas filled with exciting opportunities, so let's seize them together.
