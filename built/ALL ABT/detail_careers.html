Senior Brand Designer

Lead identity systems from first sketch to launch, partnering with strategists and motion designers across our most ambitious client work.

You will own brand projects end to end — shaping the core idea, building flexible visual systems, and guiding them into the wild across digital, print, and motion. We are looking for a designer with five or more years of identity experience, a sharp typographic eye, and the rare ability to defend a bold idea without bulldozing the room. You will work alongside a small, senior team where craft is the standard and shipping is the habit.
