Lumen Coffee Rebrand

©2025
Branding
Packaging
Web Design
A full identity and packaging system for a specialty roaster opening its first flagship café.

Lumen came to us with great beans and no story. We built one — a warm, confident identity rooted in the ritual of the morning cup. From the wordmark to the bag, every detail was designed to feel handmade but never precious, scaling cleanly from a single espresso label to a citywide rollout. The result lifted shelf recognition and gave the team a brand they were proud to pour.

We wanted the brand to feel like sunrise in a cup — quiet, warm, and impossible to ignore once you noticed it.
From bean to billboard, every touchpoint speaks the same warm, confident language.
