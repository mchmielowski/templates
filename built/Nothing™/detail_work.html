Hollow

A quiet brand system for a studio that lets the work speak

Things we did
Brand identity
build
Art direction
Client
Hollow Studio
Year
2026
Live website
Visit website
Hollow makes furniture you’re meant to ignore until you need it. The brand had to match: confident, unfussy, and quiet enough to put the products first. We built an identity and a site that get out of the way.
Mobile experience
Crafting Elements

We started with the parts you barely notice: a single typeface, two weights, and a grid that never argues with the content. Every component was drawn to hold a product photo without competing with it, so the catalogue could grow without the system ever feeling full.

branding

The mark is a hollow square — present, but mostly negative space. It reads at a favicon and holds up on a delivery van, and it never asks to be the hero. That restraint became the rule for everything else: if an element had to shout to be seen, it didn’t earn its place.

A lighter brand turned out to be a measurable one — quieter pages, clearer numbers.
+38%
Increase in checkout completion after launch
0.9s
Median load time across the new product pages

Hollow carries a distinct softness — but it’s not fragile.

“They gave us a brand that knows when to be quiet. Sales went up the moment the noise went down.”
Daniela Ferro
Founder, Hollow Studio
Navigation Explorations
Mobile Design

On phones the navigation collapses to a single, honest menu — no hamburger gymnastics, no hidden gestures to learn. Product pages stack into one clean column so the imagery stays large and the checkout stays one thumb away. Quiet on desktop, quieter on mobile.
