The Power of Clarity Over Confidence

6 min read
·
by
Maya Lindqvist

We tend to confuse confidence with clarity. Confidence says, "I know I can do this." Clarity says, "I know what this is, and I know the next true step." One is a feeling that comes and goes with the weather of a day. The other is a practice you can return to even when you feel uncertain, tired, or unsure.

When I stopped waiting to feel confident before starting, my work changed. I began asking smaller, more honest questions: What is actually in front of me right now? What can I let go of today? Clarity rarely arrives as a thunderclap. It accumulates in quiet moments — in the pause before a decision, in the willingness to name what you do not yet understand.

So the next time you find yourself stalled, don't reach for more confidence. Reach for clarity instead. Slow down enough to see the shape of the thing you're holding. Confidence will follow, eventually — but it was never the part that mattered most.
