What’s included

Made for people who make space

60-minute video call
A focused session to speak things out loud, with space to pause, unpack, and reframe what’s on your mind.
Thoughtful pre-session intake
A gentle form to get to know your context, intentions, and where you’re currently standing — so we start with clarity.
Voice check-ins between sessions
Short audio messages to share thoughts, wins, or questions between calls — for when ideas surface unannounced.
Personalized follow-up notes
After each session, you’ll receive a quiet summary — reflections, themes, and prompts to take with you.
