About me

A quiet structure for thoughtful brands

A Quick Hello
I design with attention — to what’s said and unsaid, to the shape of silence, to the way people move through spaces and stories.

&Fold™ isn’t just a studio. It’s a quiet structure for thoughtful brands to take shape in a grounded, lasting way.

I work with emerging businesses, solo practitioners, and small collectives who want to lead with clarity and care.

Sometimes we make a brand from scratch. Sometimes we reorganize what’s already there — and let it breathe.

This isn’t about trends. It’s about resonance.

Because when your brand feels like yours, it works better for everyone.

FAQ

Common  questions

Do I need to know how to code to use &Fold™?

Not at all. Everything’s built in Webflow — clean, modular, and ready to go. You can edit it without touching a line of code.

Can I customize the fonts, colors, and layout?

Yes — completely. You can adapt the whole visual system to match your brand’s voice and vibe, no stress.

Is it responsive on all devices?

Yes. &Fold™ is crafted to flow beautifully across desktop, tablet, and mobile — without breakage or weird shifts.

What’s included with the purchase?

You’ll get the full Webflow template. If you want the Figma file, just head to byq.studio, drop us your order confirmation, and we’ll send it over.

Can I use it for more than one project?

Each license is valid for one website. If you’re planning to use it for multiple clients or brands, we ask that you purchase additional licenses.

What if I get stuck?

We don’t offer full support, but if you hit a wall — reach out. We’re happy to help with small questions or point you in the right direction.
