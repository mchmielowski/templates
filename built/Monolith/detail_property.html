The Embarcadero Penthouse
88 Steuart Street, San Francisco, CA
More photos
More photos
More photos
More photos
A sculptural residence above the bay
Description

Perched on the top two floors of a landmark waterfront tower, this penthouse pairs board-formed concrete with floor-to-ceiling glass framing the Bay Bridge and open water beyond. A private elevator opens onto an entry gallery; the living level flows uninterrupted to a 900-square-foot terrace. Every material was chosen to age gracefully — honed limestone, smoked oak, and blackened steel — for a home that feels as enduring as the city skyline it overlooks.

Amenities
Private elevator
Property history
2019 — Completed

Designed by Mira Voss Architects and topped out in 2019, the tower set a new benchmark for waterfront living in the Financial District.

2020 — First Owner

Acquired by a private collector who commissioned the bespoke smoked-oak millwork and integrated lighting throughout.

2023 — Renovated

A full interior refresh introduced honed limestone floors, a chef's kitchen, and a climate-controlled wine room.

2026 — Listed

Offered for sale for the first time, presented in turnkey condition with all custom furnishings negotiable.
