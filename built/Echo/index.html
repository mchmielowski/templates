More power. Better results.

Overview of all the features included in Echo Studio Agency Template.

Premium, custom, simply great

Yes, we know... it's easy to say it, but that's the fact. We did put a lot of thought into the template. Echo was designed by an award-winning designer. Layouts you will find in our template are custom made to fit the industry after carefully made research.

Optimised for speed

We used our best practices to make sure your new website loads fast. All of the images are compressed to have as little size as possible. Whenever possible we used vector formats - the format made for the web.

Responsive

Echo Template is optimized to offer a frictionless experience on every screen. No matter how you combine our sections, they will look good on desktop, tablet, and phone.

Reusable animations

Both complex and simple animations are an inseparable element of modern website. We created our animations in a way that can be easily reused, even by beginners.

Modular

Our template is modular, meaning you can combine different sections as well as single elements, like buttons, images, etc. with each other without losing on consistency of the design. Long story short, different elements will always look good together.

100% customisable

On top of being modular, Echo was created using the best techniques, like: global Color Swatches, reusable classes, symbols and more.

CMS

Echo includes a blog, tags, projects and careers collections that are made on the powerful CMS. This will let you add new content extremely easily.

Ecommerce

Echo Agency Template comes with eCommerce set up, so you can start selling your services straight away.

Figma included

To give you 100% control over the design, together with project, you also get the Figma file.
