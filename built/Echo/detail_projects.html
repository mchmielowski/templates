Lumen — Rebranding a Solar Startup

Brand Identity

2026

Lumen came to Echo with a great product and an identity that was holding it back — a cool but generic mark that looked like every other clean-energy company on the market. Our brief was to make a fast-growing solar startup feel as warm and human as the future it was selling, without losing the credibility that wins enterprise contracts.

We rebuilt the brand around a single idea: light that belongs to everyone. A custom wordmark, a sunrise-inspired palette and a flexible system of radial graphics gave Lumen a look that scales from a rooftop installer's van to an investor deck. Alongside the visual work we wrote a new voice — plain, optimistic and free of jargon — and rolled it out across the website, app onboarding and field sales materials.

In the first quarter after launch, branded search for Lumen rose 63%, demo requests nearly doubled, and the sales team finally had a story that felt as ambitious as the company itself.
