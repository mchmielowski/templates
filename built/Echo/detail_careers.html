Senior Brand Designer

We are looking for a Senior Brand Designer to lead identity work for some of the most ambitious clients on our roster. You will own projects end to end — from the first moodboard to the final guidelines — shaping how startups and established brands show up in the world.

You will partner closely with strategists, motion designers and developers, set the creative direction for your projects, and mentor mid-level designers as the studio grows. We care less about where you trained and more about the quality of your thinking and the craft in your work.

You should have 5+ years of agency or in-house experience, a portfolio of brand systems you can talk through in depth, fluency in Figma and the Adobe suite, and a genuine love for type, colour and the small details that make identities sing.
