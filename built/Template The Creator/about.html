Milestones

My quick

story

2015
First Camera, First Love

At 16, I picked up my first DSLR camera. It was an old hand-me-down, but it opened up a world I never knew existed. From capturing sunsets to random street shots, that’s when I realized photography was my happy place.

2017
Instagram Goes Live

Two years later, I took a leap and started my Instagram page. No fancy content, just a girl sharing her world. Little did I know that what started as a hobby would grow into something much bigger — connecting with thousands of people who shared my love.

2019
The First Paid Gig

This was a game-changer. I was 20 when I landed my first paid gig with a local brand that saw potential in my content. It wasn’t just about the money; it was the moment I realized I could turn my passion into a career.

2021
Expanding Horizons

At 22, I branched out beyond Instagram, launching my YouTube channel and blog. This was the year I became a full-fledged content creator, sharing not just pretty pictures but stories, guides, and tips that helped my community.

2022
1 Million Followers!

I hit the big 1M on Instagram! This was such a surreal moment. I celebrated with my dog Francis, of course, who has been a huge part of my journey (and content). That milestone opened doors to collaborations with global brands and solidified my spot in the world of content creators.

2023
Launched My Own Merch

Last year, I decided to take my brand to the next level by launching my own line of merch. From design to production, it was a crazy learning experience, but seeing people wear something I created? Pure magic.

“Every journey starts with a single snapshot — and I’m here to capture every moment along the way.”
