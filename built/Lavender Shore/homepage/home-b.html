FAQ

Common  
questions

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is available from 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM on your departure day.

Is breakfast included in the stay?

Yes. Each morning begins with a seasonal, plant-forward breakfast served in our garden kitchen or directly to your villa — your choice.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. We offer private car transfers from and to Ngurah Rai International Airport. Just let us know your flight details — we’ll take care of the rest.

Are children allowed at the resort?

We welcome guests aged 12 and older. To preserve the quiet, reflective nature of the property, we do not accommodate small children.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Every villa and common space has secure, fast Wi-Fi — though most of our guests forget to ask for the password.

Can I host an event or retreat at the resort?

Yes. We love hosting intimate retreats, workshops and gatherings.Please contact us directly — we’ll help you design an experience that feels right.
