Monkey Adventure

Fridays at dusk
Limited to 8 guests
reserve your spot
reserve your spot

As the sun slips behind the trees, a different kind of magic begins. Our Monkey Adventure leads you deep into the jungle just before nightfall — when the forest feels most alive. Guided by a local with stories older than the island’s temples, you’ll follow hidden footpaths beneath swaying canopies, where every rustle might be a monkey, and every shadow holds a secret.

‍

This isn’t a tourist trail. It’s a slow, mindful walk into the quieter parts of Bali’s soul. You’ll meet long-tailed macaques in their natural home, observe their rituals, their chaos, their calm. There’s no stage here — just nature doing its thing, and us trying to listen.

‍

After the walk, we gather near the edge of the forest, where tea is warm, conversation is slow, and the sky turns lavender above the trees. The group is always small — never more than 8 — because the moment deserves space.

‍

Whether you’re here for the monkeys, the stillness, or the dusk itself, this is your invitation to get a little lost, then found again.

Duration
Around 2.5 hours
Meeting Point
By the stone lanterns near the garden gate, 5:45 PM sharp.
What’s included
  • Guided twilight walk through the monkey forest
  • Local guide with deep knowledge of the area
  • Natural snacks and herbal tea afterwards
  • Quiet time by the forest edge at sunset
Notes
Bring quiet shoes, an open heart, and something to cover your shoulders. We keep it small and mindful — just 8 guests.
Sign up for the experience
We’ll get back to you within 24 hours. If you prefer, feel free to email us directly at halo@thelavendershore.com or message @thelavendershore on Instagram.
