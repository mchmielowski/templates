The Pavilion

4 guests
All Inclusive
A quiet space hidden between trees and light. The Orchid Villa was designed for the slowest kind of stay — one where you forget your phone exists. High ceilings, soft linens, open-air bathroom, and just enough room to move, breathe, and do nothing.

We built this villa as a space to disconnect — not from people, but from pressure. There’s something about the way the light moves through the open bathroom in the morning that just… slows you down.

Most of our guests tell us they end up staying in more than they expected. That’s exactly the point. The plunge pool is private enough for a morning dip with coffee, and the stone path down to the main garden only takes 30 seconds barefoot.

If you’re staying during the full moon, ask us for the incense bowl. Trust us.
