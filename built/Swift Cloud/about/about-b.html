Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started

Sign up at swift.cloud/start and our onboarding wizard walks you through workspace setup in under 10 minutes. A dedicated Customer Success manager is assigned to every new account to guide your first 30 days.

What security measures are in place to protect my data?

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We undergo annual SOC 2 Type II audits, offer optional SSO and MFA, and maintain full GDPR and CCPA compliance. Your data is never shared with third parties.

Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Yes. Swift Cloud connects to 200+ popular tools including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Jira, and Google Workspace via our REST API and native connectors. Custom webhooks are available on all paid plans.

How does your 24/7 customer support work?

Our support team operates around the clock via live chat and email. Enterprise customers also get a dedicated phone line and a named support engineer. Average first-response time is under 4 minutes.

How does your 30-day free trial work?"

Your 30-day trial gives you full access to all features with no credit card required. At the end of the trial you can choose any paid plan, or your workspace moves to our free tier automatically.

Is customer support available for all subscription plans?

Yes — live chat and email support are available on every plan. Phone support and a dedicated support engineer are included on Professional and Enterprise plans.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription plan?

Absolutely. Plan changes take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your account settings — no phone calls or contracts required.
