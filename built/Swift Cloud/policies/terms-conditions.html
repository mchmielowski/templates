Terms & Conditions

Swift Cloud ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use, store, and protect it. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to the practices described here. We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, company name, and payment details — as well as technical data gathered automatically when you use our services, including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and session duration. All information collected is used solely to operate, improve, and secure our platform and to communicate with you about your account, billing, and product updates.

‍

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@swiftcloud.io. We respond to all verifiable requests within 30 days. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Any third-party service providers we use are contractually required to handle data in accordance with this policy and applicable data protection laws.

How We Use Your Information

‍

We use the information we collect to provide and maintain our cloud platform, process transactions, send transactional and product-related communications, and respond to your support requests. We analyse aggregated, anonymised usage data to identify performance bottlenecks and prioritise new features. We do not use your data for targeted advertising, nor do we allow advertising networks access to your account information.

‍

‍

We may also use your information to enforce our Terms of Service, detect and prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and protect the rights, property, and safety of Swift Cloud and its users. Where we process data based on legitimate interests, you have the right to object at any time by contacting privacy@swiftcloud.io.

Data Security Measures

‍

We implement industry-leading technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information. All data transmitted to and from Swift Cloud is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256. Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel using multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls. We conduct annual penetration tests performed by independent third-party security firms, and we undergo SOC 2 Type II audits each year. Security incidents are investigated immediately; affected customers are notified within 72 hours of confirmed breach discovery in accordance with applicable regulations. We maintain a formal vulnerability disclosure programme — security researchers may report findings at security@swiftcloud.io. Backup data is encrypted and stored in geographically separate data centres to ensure business continuity.

‍

‍

‍

Third-Party Sharing

‍

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers — including cloud hosting, payment processing, and analytics partners — solely to help us operate our platform. All such providers are contractually obligated to use data only as directed by us and to maintain appropriate security standards. We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Swift Cloud, our users, or the public. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of our assets, user information may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the same privacy protections outlined here.
