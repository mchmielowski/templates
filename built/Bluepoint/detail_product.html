Description

A relaxed-fit tee in heavyweight organic cotton, printed in the studio with our signature Bluepoint mark. Designed to be worn into the ground and still look good.

Product details

100% organic ringspun cotton, 240gsm. Unisex sizing XS–XXL. Water-based screen print. Ethically manufactured and shipped in plastic-free packaging.

Use instruction

For the best fit, size up if you prefer a looser drape. The cotton will soften with every wash and settle into its final shape after the first wear.

Washing & care

Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colours. Hang to dry and avoid ironing directly over the print. No bleach.

FAQ

Answers to frequently asked questions.
What services do you offer?

We are happy to talk this through in more detail. Drop us a line at hello@bluepointagency.com and we will walk you through exactly how it works for a project like yours.

How long does a project take?

A focused brand sprint runs around four weeks. A full identity with packaging and a website typically takes eight to twelve weeks, depending on the number of stakeholders and approval rounds. We share a clear timeline before any work begins.

Can you work with my budget?

Often, yes. We offer fixed-scope packages for smaller teams and flexible retainers for ongoing work. Tell us what you are working with and we will shape the scope to fit rather than pad it.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes. Every project ends with a handoff session and documentation, and we offer monthly support retainers for teams that want us on call for new assets, campaigns, or tweaks after launch.
