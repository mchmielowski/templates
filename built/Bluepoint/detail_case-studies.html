Northwind Coffee — a roaster's rebrand

Year
2026
Services
Brand strategy
Visual identity
Packaging design
Signage & environment
Art direction

Northwind began as a single neighbourhood roastery with a loyal following and no room to grow. As the team opened new locations, the original hand-painted logo stopped scaling and the look felt inconsistent from cafe to bag to website. We were asked to build an identity that could travel — warm enough for a corner shop, sharp enough for a national rollout. The result is a flexible wordmark, a grounded earth-toned palette, and a packaging system that lets every origin feel distinct while still reading unmistakably as Northwind.

The challenge

A beloved local roaster outgrowing its hand-drawn beginnings. The brand needed room to expand across cities without losing the warmth that made customers loyal in the first place — and a packaging system simple enough for a small team to extend on their own.

The outcome

A modular identity that rolled out across five locations, a full retail bag range, and a refreshed online store in under three months. Six weeks after launch, wholesale enquiries were up 40% and the new packaging had become the most-tagged detail on the brand's social feed.
