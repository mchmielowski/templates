Frequently asked questions

How do I get started

Getting started takes under 10 minutes. Sign up for a free 30-day trial, connect your data sources with our guided setup wizard, and your first dashboard will be live before your coffee cools. Our onboarding team is available via live chat every step of the way.

What security measures are in place to protect my data?

We use AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Our infrastructure runs in SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified data centres, with role-based access controls, automated vulnerability scanning, and quarterly third-party penetration tests to keep your data protected at every layer.

Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Yes — Future Studio connects natively with 200+ tools including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Jira, and all major cloud providers via REST API and pre-built webhooks. Our integration library is updated monthly, and our engineering team builds custom connectors for enterprise clients on request.

How does your 24/7 customer support work?

Every plan includes round-the-clock support via live chat and email. Business and Enterprise subscribers also receive a dedicated Customer Success Manager and a guaranteed 1-hour response SLA. We publish a comprehensive self-service knowledge base and host weekly live Q&A webinars open to all subscribers.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription plan?

Absolutely. Switch plans at any time from your account dashboard — upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle. Any unused credit from your current plan is prorated automatically, so you never pay twice for the same period.
