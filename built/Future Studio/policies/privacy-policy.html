Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Future Studio collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our services. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how we handle it. By using Future Studio, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

‍

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, fill out a contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, or reach out to customer support. We also collect data automatically as you interact with the platform, including log data, device identifiers, usage metrics, and cookies that help us improve your experience.

How We Use Your Information

‍

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve Future Studio; to send transactional emails such as account confirmations and billing receipts; to deliver personalised content and product recommendations; to conduct analytics that inform our product roadmap; and to comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

‍

‍

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You may also object to certain processing activities, request data portability, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact our Privacy team at privacy@futurestudio.io. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Security Measures

‍

We implement industry-standard security measures — including end-to-end AES-256 encryption, regular independent security audits, and strict role-based access controls — to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our infrastructure is hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centres monitored around the clock.

‍

‍

‍

Third-Party Sharing

‍

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share it with carefully vetted service providers who assist us in operating the platform (such as cloud hosting, payment processing, and email delivery), but only under strict data processing agreements that prohibit them from using your data for their own purposes. We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
