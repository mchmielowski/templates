Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Grained collects, uses, and protects personal information provided by visitors and customers of our website and services. We are committed to handling your data responsibly, transparently, and in full compliance with applicable data protection regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. We review and update this document periodically; the date of the most recent revision appears at the top of the page. Please check back regularly to stay informed of any changes to how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when it is necessary to deliver our services or improve your experience. The categories of information we may collect include contact details you provide through enquiry forms, technical data collected automatically when you visit our site, and project-related information shared with us during an engagement.

  • Name, email address, and phone number provided via contact or enquiry forms.
  • Device and browser information, IP address, and approximate location data collected automatically when you visit our site to help us diagnose issues and improve performance.
  • Details of project enquiries, preferences, and correspondence shared with us during a client engagement.
  • Payment information processed securely by our third-party payment provider; we do not store card details on our own servers.

‍

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to enquiries, deliver our design and development services, and communicate with you about your project. We may also use anonymised usage data to analyse website performance and identify opportunities to improve the experience for all visitors.

Where you have opted in to marketing communications, we may send you updates about new services, case studies, or industry insights. You can withdraw this consent at any time by following the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly at the address listed on our Contact page.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties for their own marketing purposes under any circumstances.

‍

Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include encrypted storage, access controls limited to authorised personnel, and regular security reviews. While no method of internet transmission is completely secure, we take every reasonable step to protect your information.

‍

Third-Party Sharing

We work with a small number of trusted third-party service providers including cloud hosting platforms, payment processors, and analytics tools. Each provider is bound by a data processing agreement and is prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than those we specify. We disclose personal data to third parties only where required by law or with your explicit consent.

‍

Your Privacy Choices and Controls

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. You may also request that we restrict processing or object to certain uses of your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address listed on our Contact page. We will respond within 30 days. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection supervisory authority.
