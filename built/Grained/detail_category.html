Let's talk
How do you approach the design and development process?

We begin every engagement with a thorough discovery phase, listening closely to your goals, researching your market, and mapping user journeys before a single pixel is placed. Our iterative process moves from wireframes through high-fidelity prototypes, with continuous feedback loops ensuring the final product is both beautiful and functionally precise.

What is the typical timeline for a project with your agency?

Most projects run between six and sixteen weeks depending on scope. A focused brand identity or landing page can be delivered in four to six weeks, while a full product design system or e-commerce build typically spans twelve to sixteen weeks. We share a detailed timeline at the start of every project so there are never any surprises.

How do you ensure the security of client data and information?

We treat your data with the same care we bring to design. All client files are stored in encrypted, access-controlled environments. We sign NDAs on request, limit data access to team members actively working on your project, and follow GDPR-compliant data handling practices throughout our engagement.

What is your pricing structure for digital services?

Our pricing is transparent and project-based. Each engagement begins with a scoping call that results in a fixed-fee proposal covering deliverables, revision rounds, and timeline. We also offer monthly retainer packages for ongoing design support. Visit our Pricing page to explore current plan options.

How can we get started with your agency for our digital needs?

Getting started is simple. Fill out the contact form on our Contact page or send us an email and we will arrange a free 30-minute discovery call. From there we will prepare a tailored proposal within 48 hours, with no commitment required until you are ready to move forward.

What sets your digital agency apart from others?

We combine strategic thinking with award-winning craft. Unlike generalist studios, every specialist at Grained works exclusively in digital design, meaning you get deep expertise rather than divided attention. Our track record spans startups, scale-ups, and global brands, and we measure success by outcomes, not just deliverables.

How can your agency help improve our online presence?

A stronger online presence starts with understanding what your audience expects and where your current experience falls short. We audit your existing digital touchpoints, identify friction, and design purposeful improvements — from clearer navigation to a faster checkout flow — that translate directly into higher engagement and conversion.

What services does your digital agency offer?

Grained offers UI/UX design, brand identity, product strategy, web and mobile development, e-commerce design, and ongoing design support retainers. Whether you need a complete digital product built from scratch or a seasoned design partner to level up an existing platform, we have a service tier that fits your goals.
