Frequently asked questions
How do I get started

Getting started is simple. Choose a consulting package that fits your needs and schedule a complimentary discovery call with one of our senior advisors. We will review your business objectives, match you with the right consultant, and have your engagement up and running within 48 hours of signing.

What security measures are in place to protect my data?

We take data security seriously. All client information is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 standards. We are fully GDPR compliant and maintain SOC 2 Type II controls. Our staff undergo regular security training, and you retain full ownership of your data throughout the engagement.

Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Yes. Our advisory approach is designed to complement your existing tools and platforms. Whether you use Salesforce, Microsoft 365, SAP, or a custom ERP system, our consultants will assess your current stack and recommend integration strategies that improve efficiency without disrupting operations.

How does your 24/7 customer support work?

All clients have access to our dedicated client portal and a direct line to their assigned advisor during business hours. For urgent matters outside standard hours, our on-call team monitors the priority channel around the clock. Critical issues are acknowledged within one hour and resolved as quickly as possible.
