Studio Notes
A look inside our creative process — from the first messy sketch to the finished identity system — and why the boldest work tends to happen when you step away from the templates everyone else is using.
Start with the question, not the logo

Every project we take on begins with a conversation, not a moodboard. Before we sketch a single mark, we want to understand what a brand is actually for — who it serves, what it believes, and the gap it fills that nobody else can. That clarity becomes the brief that everything else answers to.

From there we move fast and loud. We test typefaces at billboard scale, run color against the worst-case backgrounds, and pressure the system until it either breaks or proves it can live anywhere. The brands we are proudest of are the ones that still feel unmistakably themselves on a tiny app icon and a building-sized mural alike.
