We are OFF THE GRID

What started as a passion project between two college friends has grown into a multinational creative agency that’s redefining what it means to build a brand. We saw the opportunity to break free from the typical, the expected, and the ordinary.
Today, OFF THE GRID spans continents and industries, but our mission remains the same: to lead with bold ideas, push creative boundaries, and help brands stand out in an overcrowded world.
OFFFFF
OFFFFF is not just a name - it’s a mindset.
We Believe
Creativity is about moving forward- always evolving, adapting, and innovating.
We Craft
Experiences that break boundaries and shape the future of brands.

From late-night brainstorming sessions in a small dorm room to working with global brands, we’ve always kept one thing at the core of our business: creativity without limits. Our journey was never about playing by the rules - it was about creating our own path, together.
