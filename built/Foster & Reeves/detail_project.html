Cedar Hollow Mixed-Use Development

Location
Portland, Oregon
Service
General Contracting

When the Cedar Hollow ownership group needed a builder who could deliver a complex urban development on a constrained budget and timeline, they turned to Foster & Reeves. What followed was an eighteen-month partnership grounded in transparency, craftsmanship, and relentless coordination.

The concrete podium and structural frame, self-performed by our crews.

Cedar Hollow brought together 84 residential units and 12,000 square feet of ground-floor retail on a tight infill lot in Portland's Eastside. Foster & Reeves served as general contractor from groundbreaking through final occupancy, coordinating a phased schedule that kept the adjacent streetscape open to local businesses throughout construction.

Testimonial
"Foster & Reeves treated our budget and our timeline like their own. They flagged risks early, solved problems before they reached us, and handed over a building we're genuinely proud of."
Daniel Okafor
Development Director, Cedar Hollow Partners

Our team self-performed the concrete podium and structural framing, allowing us to compress the early schedule by nearly five weeks. Tight coordination between trades and a just-in-time delivery plan kept the cramped site organized and safe, even at peak crew counts of 120 workers.

Ground-floor retail bays framed for daylight and street activation.
The finished courtyard, completed two weeks ahead of schedule.

Sustainability was central from day one. We hit a 92% construction-waste diversion rate, installed a 60kW rooftop solar array, and detailed the envelope to exceed Oregon energy code by 18 percent. The completed building earned LEED Gold certification on first submission.
