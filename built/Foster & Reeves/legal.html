Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 1, 2025
Introduction
Welcome to Foster & Reeves. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your personal information when you use our website. Your privacy is a priority, and we are committed to safeguarding your data.
INFORMATION WE COLLECT
Personal Data
Your name, email address, and any contact details you voluntarily provide through our forms.
Usage Data
Information on how you navigate and interact with our site, which is analyzed to enhance our services.
User-Provided Content
Any messages, files, or other data you voluntarily submit to us through the website.
HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
  • To operate and maintain our website and services.
  • To send you relevant communications and administrative updates.
  • To analyze usage data to improve our website and offerings.
DISCLOSURE OF YOUR INFORMATION
Your data is accessible only by authorized Foster & Reeves personnel.
‍
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Third-party partners who assist in maintaining our site are bound by strict confidentiality and security agreements.
DATA RETENTION

We retain your data for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes for which it was collected,
  • Or to comply with your request for its removal, subject to legal obligations.
SECURITY OF YOUR INFORMATION
We employ administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information. While we take reasonable precautions, no system is completely secure.
YOUR RIGHTS
You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. Please contact us to exercise these rights.
Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us. Foster & Reeves is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring our practices are transparent and fair. You can reach our data protection officer at privacy@fosterreeves.com or by mail at: Foster & Reeves Corp., 789 Innovation Blvd., Greendale, WA 98052.
